Check your tenancy agreement. Prove the version you signed.

Not legal advice. SewaCheck is a screening tool for Malaysian residential tenancies. It uses transparent rules based on the Contracts Act 1950, Specific Relief Act 1950, Distress Act 1951, Civil Law Act 1956, Stamp Act 1949 and common market practice. As of Oct 2026 the Residential Tenancy Act is still a draft Bill — not tabled or in force — so your agreement plus general law is what counts. Sources

      2 Fingerprint & anchor Cap jari & rekod

      Once both sides agree on the final text, SewaCheck computes its SHA-256 fingerprint on your phone. Change one comma and the fingerprint changes completely. Anchoring the fingerprint on Ethereum gives a public, timestamped proof of which version was agreed — without putting the agreement itself on-chain.
