ROOM RENTAL AGREEMENT

[SAMPLE FOR TESTING ONLY - all names and addresses are fictitious. This sample deliberately has no clause numbers, to show paragraph-based splitting.]

This room rental agreement is made between Encik Hafiz bin Osman (the Owner) and Ms Chong Pei Yi (the Tenant) for the Middle Room at No. 12, Jalan SS15/Contoh, 47500 Subang Jaya, Selangor.

The term of the tenancy is twelve (12) months starting 1 December 2026. The monthly rent is RM750.00 per month, inclusive of water and internet, payable on the 1st of every month.

The Tenant shall pay a security deposit of RM1,500.00 and a utility deposit of RM375.00 before moving in. The security deposit will be refunded within thirty (30) days after the Tenant moves out, less any unpaid rent and the cost of repairing damage caused by the Tenant.

Electricity for the room's air-conditioner is metered separately and charged at the actual TNB rate shown on the bill.

If the Tenant leaves before the end of 12 months, the security deposit will be forfeited.

The Owner may enter the room at any time for inspection.

The Owner may change the house rules from time to time without notice to the Tenant. The current house rules (no smoking, quiet hours after 11pm, guests to leave by 10pm) are attached.

Late rent will be charged interest at 10% per annum.

The Tenant shall keep the room clean and return it in the same condition, fair wear and tear excepted. An inventory of the furniture in the room is attached.

This agreement shall be stamped within 30 days and the stamp duty shall be paid by the Tenant.
