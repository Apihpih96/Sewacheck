TERM AND RENT
1. The term of the tenancy shall be one (1) year commencing 1 November 2026.
2. The monthly rent shall be RM2,000.00 per month, payable on or before the 7th day of each month by bank transfer or DuitNow.
3. If any rent remains unpaid for more than seven (7) days after its due date, the Tenant shall pay interest on the overdue amount at the rate of 8% per annum calculated on a daily basis until full payment.

DEPOSITS
4. The Tenant shall pay a security deposit of RM4,000.00 (equivalent to two (2) months' rent) and a utility deposit of RM1,000.00 upon signing this Agreement.
5. The deposits shall be refunded to the Tenant, free of interest, within fourteen (14) days after the Tenant delivers vacant possession, less only lawful deductions for unpaid rent, unpaid utility bills and the reasonable cost of repairing damage beyond fair wear and tear, supported by an itemised statement and receipts.
6. The Tenant shall not use the deposits as payment of rent without the Landlord's written consent.

TENANT'S OBLIGATIONS
7. The Tenant shall pay for electricity, water, sewerage and internet consumed during the tenancy. Meter readings shall be recorded on the handover date.
8. The Tenant shall keep the interior of the Premises in good and tenantable repair and shall be responsible for minor repairs not exceeding RM200 per item and for any damage caused by the Tenant's negligence, fair wear and tear excepted.
9. The Tenant shall not sublet the Premises without the Landlord's written consent.
10. Upon expiry, the Tenant shall deliver up the Premises in the same good and tenantable condition as at commencement, as recorded in the inventory and photographs, fair wear and tear excepted.

LANDLORD'S OBLIGATIONS
11. The Landlord shall pay the quit rent, assessment, maintenance charges, sinking fund and fire insurance for the Premises.
12. The Landlord shall be responsible for structural repairs, roof and waterproofing, main wiring and piping, and the repair or replacement of the Landlord's appliances that fail through normal use.
13. The Landlord may enter the Premises at reasonable hours to inspect or carry out repairs upon giving the Tenant at least twenty-four (24) hours' prior written notice, save in an emergency.
14. The Landlord shall permit the Tenant to peaceably hold and enjoy the Premises during the term without interruption.

TERMINATION AND RENEWAL
15. Either party may terminate this Agreement after the first six (6) months by giving two (2) months' prior written notice.
16. If the Tenant remains in occupation after expiry without the Landlord's consent, the Tenant shall pay double the monthly rent for the period of holding over, pursuant to section 28(4)(a) of the Civil Law Act 1956.
17. The Landlord shall recover possession only by proceedings in court in accordance with section 7(2) of the Specific Relief Act 1950.
18. The Tenant may renew this Agreement for a further one (1) year by written notice at least two (2) months before expiry, at a rent to be mutually agreed, with any increase not exceeding 10%.

GENERAL
19. The stamp duty on this Agreement shall be borne by the Tenant, and the Agreement shall be stamped via e-Duti Setem within thirty (30) days of signing. Each party shall bear its own solicitors' costs.
20. The Landlord shall use the Tenant's personal data only for administering this tenancy.
21. No variation of this Agreement shall be effective unless made in writing and signed by both parties.
