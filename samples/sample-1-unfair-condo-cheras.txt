TERM AND RENT
1. The term of the tenancy shall be two (2) years commencing 1 November 2026.
2. The monthly rent shall be RM1,800.00 per month, payable in advance on or before the 1st day of each month.
3. If any rent is not paid on the due date, the Tenant shall pay a late payment charge of RM50.00 per day and interest at 3% per month on the outstanding sum until full payment.

DEPOSITS
4. The Tenant shall pay a security deposit of RM5,400.00 (equivalent to three (3) months' rent) and a utility deposit of RM1,800.00 upon signing. The utility deposit is non-refundable.
5. If the Tenant breaches any term of this Agreement, the entire security deposit shall be forfeited absolutely to the Landlord.
6. The deposits shall be returned to the Tenant after the Landlord is satisfied that all obligations have been performed.

TENANT'S OBLIGATIONS
7. The Tenant shall be responsible for all repairs and maintenance of the Premises whatsoever, including structural repairs, roof leaks, concealed pipes and main wiring, and shall replace any air-conditioner or water heater which breaks down howsoever caused.
8. The Tenant shall pay all outstanding electricity, water and Indah Water bills and maintenance charges for the Premises including any arrears incurred before the commencement of the tenancy.
9. The Tenant shall pay the quit rent, assessment and maintenance fees for the Premises during the term.
10. Upon expiry, the Tenant shall deliver up the Premises in original condition and shall repaint the entire unit at the Tenant's own cost.

LANDLORD'S RIGHTS
11. The Landlord and its agents may enter the Premises at any time without notice to inspect or show the Premises to prospective buyers.
12. If the rent is in arrears for seven (7) days, the Landlord may forthwith change the locks, cut off the electricity and water supply and remove the Tenant's belongings from the Premises without any court order.
13. The Landlord may seize and sell the Tenant's goods and furniture in the Premises to recover any rent arrears or outstanding sums.
14. The Landlord may terminate this Agreement at any time for any reason by giving seven (7) days' notice to the Tenant.
15. The Landlord may increase the monthly rent at any time during the term at its sole discretion by giving notice to the Tenant.
16. The Landlord may amend these terms and the house rules from time to time at its sole discretion without the Tenant's consent.
17. If the Tenant defaults, the Landlord may publish the Tenant's name, photograph and identity card details on social media and to any third party.

TERMINATION
18. If the Tenant terminates this tenancy before the expiry of the term for any reason, the Tenant shall pay the rent for the remaining term of the tenancy in full and the security deposit shall be forfeited.
19. Upon expiry, this Agreement shall automatically be renewed for a further two (2) years and the Landlord may increase the rent by 15%.
20. If the Tenant fails to vacate upon expiry or termination, the Tenant shall pay triple the monthly rent for every month of holding over.

GENERAL
21. The Tenant hereby waives all rights to commence any legal proceedings or claim against the Landlord in any court in respect of this tenancy. Any claim by the Tenant must be made within seven (7) days of the event giving rise to it.
22. The Landlord shall not be liable for any loss or damage to the Tenant howsoever caused, including the negligence of the Landlord.
23. The Tenant shall bear all legal costs and solicitors' fees of the Landlord on a solicitor-and-client basis in any event, whether or not any action is commenced.
